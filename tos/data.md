Such use of the term is strictly an in-game expression and does not constitute an administrative server ban.

An administrative ban is a restriction imposed by Server staff preventing a player from accessing the Server.

Players may "ban" other players as part of ordinary gameplay, including through PvP, faction conflicts, wars, rivalries, or other player-created events.

## 6. Griefing and Property Damage

Griefing is permitted where it forms a legitimate part of gameplay, conflict, competition, or player-created storytelling.

Permitted conduct may include, without limitation:

* Damaging an opposing faction's property during a conflict.
* Destroying structures belonging to an opposing player.
* Sabotaging another faction.
* Destroying or taking resources during a conflict.
* Attacking bases or infrastructure belonging to an opposing group.
* Using traps or other mechanisms against opposing players.

However, players must not engage in indiscriminate or purposeless destruction primarily intended to disrupt the Server or prevent other players from reasonably participating.

The following may constitute prohibited griefing:

* Mass destruction of unrelated builds without a gameplay-related purpose.
* Repeatedly destroying another player's property solely to frustrate or drive them from the Server.
* Destroying large amounts of infrastructure unrelated to an existing conflict.
* Deliberately attempting to make areas of the Server permanently unusable.
* Continuing destructive conduct solely for harassment after the underlying gameplay conflict has ended.

The distinction between permitted and prohibited griefing will be determined based on the circumstances and apparent purpose of the conduct.
